October 2023 · NetSupport

An entire datacenter migrated over a weekend

In 2023 we carried out one of the most challenging projects of recent years: the complete migration of a client's datacenter — servers, storage, networks and services — together with their office relocation. The window: one weekend. On Friday night users logged off as on any other day; on Monday morning they started working normally, unaware that everything supporting them had moved. Behind it were months of planning, testing, contingency plans and a team working against the clock. Because a successful migration is exactly that: the one nobody notices.
